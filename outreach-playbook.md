# Outreach Playbook — Ruchil Sharma (PM II @ Uber → roles abroad)

A practical guide to using this job board, finding the *right* recruiter, and getting replies.
Geographies in priority order: **London → scenic Europe → Dubai → US → remote founding roles.**

---

## 1. How the board works

- Open **`index.html`** (double-click). Filter by role type (Product / Ops / Founding), region, and visa status. Search any keyword.
- **"Find recruiter"** on each card opens a LinkedIn people-search pre-filled for that company + the right recruiter type. This always shows the *current* recruiter — names rotate, so this beats a static list.
- **"Outreach note"** copies a message tailored to that role. Personalize the `[brackets]` before sending.
- **`jobs.csv`** → import to Google Sheets to track outreach (Status / Date / Recruiter name / Response / Next step columns are ready).

> On recruiter names: I deliberately did **not** invent specific recruiter names — sending a note to the wrong/ex-employee burns the lead. The LinkedIn links + the verified contacts I *did* find (e.g. N26's candidate inbox, the named startup founders) are the reliable route.

---

## 2. Finding the right recruiter in 60 seconds

1. Click **Find recruiter** on the card (or search LinkedIn manually).
2. Narrow with these search patterns:
   - `"<Company>" recruiter product`
   - `"<Company>" "talent acquisition" product`
   - `"<Company>" technical recruiter` (for eng-heavy orgs)
   - For startups: search the **founder/hiring manager**, not a recruiter.
3. Prefer someone whose title mentions **Product / Tech / GTM** and whose location matches the role's city.
4. Use **LinkedIn filters**: Current company = the company, Location = the target city.
5. No recruiter visible? Message the **hiring manager** (search `"<Company>" "Group Product Manager"` / `"Director of Product"`) — often more effective than recruiters.

**Best channels, in order:** (1) warm intro via your network, (2) the company's actual recruiter on LinkedIn, (3) hiring manager, (4) careers-page application + a follow-up note, (5) generic talent inbox.

## 3. Templates

### A) LinkedIn connection note (<300 characters)
> Hi [First name] — Product Manager II at Uber here. Keen on the [role] at [Company]; I've shipped high-scale marketplace product/ops at Uber and am exploring roles abroad. Would love to connect and learn about your hiring plans. Thanks! – Ruchil

### B) Recruiter InMail / email
> Hi [First name],
>
> I'm Ruchil Sharma, a Product Manager II at Uber. I came across the **[role]** at **[Company]** and it's exactly the problem space I want to go deeper on.
>
> At Uber I [drove X — e.g. led the [feature/program] that improved [metric] by [Y%]], working across product, ops, and data on a high-scale marketplace. [Company]'s work on [their product/mission] resonates strongly with that.
>
> I'm actively exploring opportunities outside India, and [Company] is at the top of my list. Would you be open to a 15-min chat, or could you point me to the right person on the hiring team?
>
> Thanks so much,
> Ruchil Sharma · [LinkedIn] · [email]

### C) Hiring-manager note (more product-specific)
> Hi [First name],
>
> I lead [area] as a PM II at Uber and have been following [Company]'s work on [specific product/launch]. I noticed the [role] opening — I've [one sharp, quantified win relevant to their problem]. I'd love to learn what success looks like in the first 6 months for this role. Open to a quick chat?
>
> Best, Ruchil

### D) Founder note (founding-team / Chief of Staff roles)
> Hi [First name],
>
> Congrats on [recent raise/launch]. I'm a PM II at Uber (marketplace product + ops at scale) and I'm looking to join an early team where I can own 0→1. The [role] at [Company] is exactly that. I [relevant builder win]. Would love 15 mins to hear where you most need leverage right now.
>
> – Ruchil

### E) Follow-up (if no reply after 5–7 days)
> Hi [First name], floating this back up in case it slipped. Still very interested in the [role] at [Company] — happy to share a short note on how I'd approach [their key challenge]. Thanks for any pointer!

## 4. Visa / relocation cheat-sheet

| Region | Main route | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **UK (London)** | Skilled Worker visa | Monzo, Revolut, Wise, Google explicitly sponsor PMs. Check the company is on the [UK sponsor register](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers). |
| **Netherlands** | Highly Skilled Migrant (kennismigrant) | Fast (~2 wks), no labour-market test. Booking.com, Adyen big sponsors. 2026 salary threshold ~€5,942/mo (30+). 30% tax ruling perk. |
| **Germany** | EU Blue Card / skilled-worker | N26, Scout24, NavVis, Delivery Hero state visa+relocation support. |
| **Switzerland** | Work permit (quota-based, harder) | Google Zurich, On, Proton, Scandit relocate senior talent. Highest comp, scenic. |
| **Dubai (UAE)** | Employer work visa (straightforward) | Tax-free. Careem (Uber-owned!), Noon, Talabat, Tabby. Quick relocation. |
| **US** | H-1B (lottery) / L-1 / O-1 | PM petitions need a "specialty occupation" angle — lean technical in JDs. **L-1 via Uber internal transfer avoids the lottery — your strongest US route.** |

## 5. Your highest-leverage moves (given you're at Uber)

1. **Uber internal transfer first.** As a current employee you may qualify for an **L-1 intra-company transfer** to the US (no H-1B lottery) or a move to **Uber Amsterdam** (HSM visa). Talk to your manager + internal mobility. This is the lowest-friction path abroad.
2. **Careem (Uber-owned, Dubai).** Same DNA, founding grocery team, tax-free, easy visa. Your Uber background is a direct advantage.
3. **Marketplace/logistics fit:** Deliveroo (London), DoorDash (US), Delivery Hero/Talabat — your Uber experience maps 1:1; lead with quantified marketplace wins.
4. **London fintech (your top geo):** Monzo, Revolut, Wise all openly sponsor PMs — apply + recruiter outreach in parallel.

---

## 6. Weekly cadence suggestion

- **Mon:** Pick 5 target roles → apply on careers page.
- **Tue–Wed:** Find recruiter + hiring manager for each → send connection notes (template A).
- **Thu:** Send 5 InMails/emails (template B/C/D).
- **Fri:** Follow up on anything 5+ days old (template E). Update `jobs.csv`.
- Aim for ~15–20 fresh touches/week; expect ~10–20% reply rate with tailored notes.
